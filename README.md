## 🎼 About the Project

Music changes with every generation. From the sounds that defined the 1950s to the songs of the 2010s, musical styles, emotions, and lyrical themes have continued to evolve. But how can we see those changes in the data?

**The Evolution of Music** is a data storytelling project that explores songs released between **1950 and 2019**. Using exploratory data analysis and compelling visualizations, we aim to uncover patterns in how music sounds, how it feels, and what its lyrics communicate across generations.

Rather than simply presenting statistics, our goal is to connect the findings into a meaningful story about musical change over time.

## 🎯 What We Aim to Achieve

- Explore how the **mood and emotional character** of music have evolved over seven decades.
- Investigate changes in **energy, danceability, and sound** over time.
- Discover how **lyrical themes** have shifted across generations.
- Compare musical trends across **different genres**.
- Communicate our findings through a **clear, engaging, and evidence-based data story**.

## 🔎 Questions We Aim to Explore

### 01 · 🎭 Has Music Become Happier or Sadder Over the Last 70 Years?

Investigate how the emotional characteristics of music have changed across generations.

### 02 · 💃 Has Music Become More Energetic and Danceable Over Time?

Examine whether songs have become more energetic and suitable for dancing across different decades.

### 03 · 🎤 How Have the Lyrical Themes of Music Changed Across Generations?

Explore how themes such as romance, sadness, and explicit language have changed over time.

### 04 · 🎸 How Has the Sound of Music Changed Over Time?

Investigate how acousticness, loudness, and instrumentalness have evolved.

### 05 · 🎼 Do Different Music Genres Follow the Same Emotional and Musical Trends?

Compare how musical characteristics have evolved across genres and generations.

## 🗓️ How We Divided the Dataset

To explore change at different points in music history, we divided the dataset into **three non-overlapping periods**:

| Period | Years | Focus |
|:---:|:---:|---|
| **01** | **1950–1979** | Establish a historical baseline for musical characteristics and themes. |
| **02** | **1980–1999** | Examine how musical sound, emotion, and lyrics changed in the following decades. |
| **03** | **2000–2019** | Explore more recent trends and compare them with earlier periods. |

We will explore the same core questions across all three periods, allowing us to compare patterns and connect our findings into one continuous story.

## 📖 The Story We Want to Tell

Our analysis follows five connected themes:

**Emotion** → **Rhythm** → **Lyrics** → **Sound** → **Genres**

Together, these themes will help us understand not only *whether* music has changed, but also *how* those changes differ across time periods and musical styles.
